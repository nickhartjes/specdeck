## 2. Host session and sharing (`crates/core`)

- [x] 2.1 `Asks` (`crates/core/src/asks.rs`, owned by the sharing loop; design decision 1): open, answer and close a read request; start, vote, reveal, vote again, stop and keep a vote; one of each at a time; answers by participant id; the host as participant 0. Verify with unit tests: a second read request is refused while one is open; a read request and a vote open together; an answer to a closed id or an old round is `Gone`; a vote can be changed and taken back until the reveal and not after; *Vote again* clears the votes and keeps the task and cards; the kept card must belong to the set.
- [x] 2.2 Deadlines: the sharing loop wakes at the earliest deadline and closes the read request with `time_up`. Verify with a test on Tokio's paused clock: a 120-second request closes at 120 seconds and not before, and *Go on* before that closes it without `time_up`.
- [x] 2.3 Per-viewer views (design decisions 3 and 4): `ReadRequest` and `Vote` on admission and on every change, with `mine` and the viewer's own question text only, `left_ms` at the time of sending, and `revealed` empty until the reveal. Protocol-5 viewers get none of it and are listed as `OlderApp` / in `older`. Verify with `crates/core/tests/sharing.rs`: two version-6 viewers and one version-5 viewer during a read request with a question and during a vote. Check that no frame to any viewer holds another participant's card before the reveal or another viewer's question text, that the version-5 viewer decodes every frame and never reconnects, and that a viewer joining after the reveal gets the revealed votes.
- [x] 2.4 Answers' rate limit (design decision 6): `Bucket` with a configurable burst and refill, `answer_buckets` by viewer id, burst 5 and 1 per second. Verify with sharing tests: a viewer who used up the request bucket with three suggestions can still vote three times; the sixth answer within a second is `RateLimited` and changes nothing; reconnecting does not refill it.
- [x] 2.5 Timeline entries (design decision 2): the sharing loop adds them through `SessionView::add_entry`, which the session's pump turns into `emit_entry`. Verify with `crates/core/tests/host_session.rs`: `ReadDone` and `Estimated` land in order between agent entries, counts only include participants present at the close who could answer, neither entry holds a name or a question, a stopped vote adds nothing, and in a terminal session the entries are emitted too.
- [x] 2.6 Reconnects and leaving: verify with sharing tests that a viewer who voted 8 and reconnects before the reveal gets `mine = 8` and is still in `voted`, and that a viewer who leaves disappears from `people` and `voted` and is back with their answer on rejoining.
- [x] 2.7 The app's viewer (`crates/core/src/viewer.rs`) passes `ReadRequest` and `Vote` to the UI as viewer events and exposes `answer_read` and `cast_vote`. Verify with a viewer test in `crates/core/tests/sharing.rs`.
- [x] 2.8 Diagnostics (design decision 9). Extend `crates/core/tests/diagnostics_leak.rs` with a marker in a read target's path and label, a task and a question's text, with Verbose logging. Verify that the leak test passes and that the events appear with their counts.
